Caring for a Sourdough Starter

A sourdough starter is a living culture of wild yeast and bacteria. With a few minutes of attention each day, a healthy starter can leaven bread for years and develop a flavour that is uniquely yours.

Daily feeding

Discard all but a small spoonful, then feed it equal weights of flour and lukewarm water. Stir well, cover loosely and leave it somewhere warm until it has doubled in size.

  • Use unbleached flour whenever possible.
  • Filtered water helps if your tap water is heavily chlorinated.
  • Keep the jar away from direct sunlight.

Signs of a healthy starter

A lively starter smells pleasantly sour, is full of bubbles and rises predictably after each feeding.
